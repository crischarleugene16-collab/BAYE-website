How the estimate works

Sales revenue − recipe ingredient cost − recorded operating expenses = estimated net operating profit. Loan payments and cash drawer payouts are not subtracted here unless entered as operating expenses. Estimated profit shows what is eligible; cash capital recovered shows only amounts you actually record above. Starting capital is not an expense.

Add a menu item and calculate its price

Enter the recipe and pack costs; the app estimates a selling price for each serving.

How to use: For each ingredient, enter the pack quantity and pack price, then the amount used per serving. For example, if a 1,000 g bag costs ₱99 and a serving uses 150 g, cost is ₱14.85. The recommended price uses the target ingredient-cost percentage; adjust it to your stall's goals. Add labor, utilities, rent, and other overhead separately when checking profitability.
